[b]Market Structure Analysis[/b]

Market Structure Analysis is a dual-mode indicator for studying confirmed swing
behavior after private market-structure pivots are formed. It can either measure
completed swing-to-swing legs or track what happens after a confirmed swing
level is touched or broken.

[b]Modes[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Leg Analysis[/b] measures completed low-to-high and high-to-low legs.
[*][b]Revisit Analysis[/b] studies what forms after a confirmed swing level is revisited or broken.
[/list]

[b]Leg Analysis[/b]
[list]
[*]Measures absolute distance from the start pivot to the end pivot.
[*]Normalizes distance by session ATR when available.
[*]Classifies each leg as bullish or bearish.
[*]Labels each leg as with trend, counter trend, broke structure, or no break.
[*]Separates in-session and out-of-session legs using the configured outside-start rule.
[/list]

[b]Revisit Analysis[/b]
[list]
[*]Stores each confirmed pivot as an active level.
[*]Opens a revisit record when price first touches or breaks that level.
[*]Waits for the next confirmed high and the next confirmed low after the break.
[*]Measures signed price distance and time distance from the break to both next swings.
[*]Shows how often the first post-break confirmed swing is a high.
[/list]

[b]Inputs[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Analysis Type[/b] switches between Leg Analysis and Revisit Analysis.
[*][b]Session / Timezone[/b] controls session membership and ATR scope.
[*][b]ATR Settings[/b] control the session ATR averaging window.
[*][b]Market Structure[/b] controls pivot lookback/lookahead and optional ATR-based confirmation.
[*][b]Table[/b] controls position and text size.
[*][b]CSV Export[/b] logs completed records to Pine Logs.
[*][b]Debug[/b] adds compact diagnostic labels for newly completed records.
[/list]

[b]CSV Output[/b]
[list]
[*]The first log line is a legend that explains each CSV column.
[*]The second log line is the raw CSV header.
[*]After that, each newly completed record is logged as one CSV row.
[*]The legend and header change automatically with the selected analysis mode.
[/list]

[b]How To Use[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Select the analysis mode you want to study.
[*]Set the session and timezone so ATR and in-session classification match your market.
[*]Adjust pivot lookback / lookahead and optional ATR confirmation.
[*]Watch the table on the last bar for aggregated results.
[*]Enable CSV Export if you want completed records in Pine Logs.
[/list]

[b]Notes[/b]
[list]
[*]Leg statistics are based on confirmed swings only, not live unconfirmed pivots.
[*]Revisit statistics complete only after both the next confirmed high and next confirmed low are known.
[*]Signed revisit distances are direction-aware: high breaks treat upward continuation as positive, low breaks treat downward continuation as positive.
[*]Session classification is asymmetric by design: starts inside are always in session, while outside starts follow the selected outside-start rule.
[/list]

[quote]
This script is intended for structural study and review. It does not place
trades, send alerts, or provide broker automation by itself.
[/quote]
